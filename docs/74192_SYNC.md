# Synchronous 74192 and the load loops

## The cell

`rtl/ttl/fonz_74192_sync.v` defines `fonz_74192_sync`, a single-clock
replacement for the event model `fonz_74192`:

```verilog
fonz_74192_sync u(.clk(clk), .up(up), .down(down), .clear(clear),
    .load_n(load_n), .d(d), .q(q), .carry_n(carry_n), .borrow_n(borrow_n));
```

The pins mean the same as on `fonz_74192`. `clk` is a free-running system
clock, not a board net. UP and DOWN are registered every `clk`; a rising
edge between the previous sample and now is applied to `q` combinationally
in the same cycle, as are CLR and /LOAD. At every `clk` edge `q`, /CARRY and
/BORROW therefore equal what the zero-delay event model shows at that
instant, and a carry or borrow that clocks the next counter (IC69 → IC91,
IC76 → IC75) settles within the same `clk`. There is no clock-to-output
latency to compensate elsewhere.

Requirement: each input changes at most once per `clk`. A pulse on UP,
DOWN, CLR or /LOAD shorter than one `clk` can be missed. The 14.305 MHz
crystal (IC45.8) or a multiple of it satisfies this for every 74192 net on
the board.

Power-up: the event model counts once at time 0 when UP starts high (its
edge history starts at 0). The sync cell starts its history high and does
not. Neither is board behaviour; the two agree from the first CLR or /LOAD.

## The loops

This was first planned when IC91 /CARRY was traced to the two /LOAD pins,
IC67B's clock and IC9's clears, which made IC69/91 reload themselves with a
zero-width pulse, and IC18 was thought to need the same. Neither loop is in
the current trace:

- IC69.11 and IC91.11 (/LOAD), IC67.9 (IC67B clock) and IC9 /CLR3,11 are
  driven by IC101.6 = NAND(IC102.5, R67/C44 node), corrected in commit
  2cca0b4 (docs/NET_CHECKS.md, "IC69/IC91 /LOAD"). The low pulse is set by
  R67 = 220 Ω and C44 = 680 pF, about 150 ns or two crystal clocks.
  IC91.12 (/CARRY) only feeds IC47.9.
- IC18 /LOAD (pin 11) is IC41.11 = AND(IC30 /Q4, IC41.13 net = ~IC83.9).
  IC30 is a 74123 half, so the pulse width is analogue, not a gate glitch,
  and IC18's outputs do not feed back to its own load.

The one real glitch-load loop is the timer, IC75/76:

```
IC75.13, IC76.13 (/BORROW) -> IC82.13 = NOR -> IC61.8 = NAND(IC83.13, R77/C52, IC82.13)
    -> IC81.6 = AND(IC101.11, IC61.8) -> IC75.11, IC76.11 (/LOAD)
```

When the time reaches 00 and the IC105 seconds clock takes IC76 DOWN low,
both borrows go low, IC81.6 loads the preset (if IC83.13 is set), the
borrows release and the load ends: a zero-width pulse in the event model.
With the sync cell this path is a combinational loop, because the cell's
flags decode `q_next` (they must, or a cascade loses counts; see below).

**Cut:** register IC82.13 on `clk` where the board is retimed, and feed the
registered copy to IC61.10, IC60.9 and IC82.9 (the IC82.10 → IC76.4 gate).
The /LOAD pulse becomes one `clk` long and each reload lands one `clk`
(70 ns) after the event model. The sequence of time values is unchanged.

Why not cut inside the cell by decoding the flags from the registered count:
that was tried. A load that moves q to 0 with DOWN already low then raises
/BORROW one clock late, after the shared /LOAD has ended, and the next
counter counts down once more. On the timer that would take a second off
IC75 at every reload, which is exactly the case the cell exists for.

## Values 10 to 15

IC91 is preset with `{IC90.7, 1, IC90.5, IC90.3}`; when IC90's /5 section
reads 4 that is 12 or 13, so IC91 regularly counts from outside 0..9.
Both cells now follow the TI SDLS074 logic diagram (SN54/74192, page 2),
read gate by gate:

| | QA | QB | QC | QD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| up toggles | 1 | QA·/QD | QA·QB | QA·QB·QC + QA·QD |
| down toggles | 1 | /QA·(QB+QC+QD) | /QA·/QB·(QC+QD) | /QA·/QB·/QC |

/CO = NAND(UP inverted, QA, QD) and /BO = NAND(DOWN inverted, /QA, /QB,
/QC, /QD). So 0..9 count as BCD, and from 10..15:

- up: 10 → 11 → 6, 12 → 13 → 4, 14 → 15 → 2; /CARRY is low at 9, 11, 13
  and 15 while UP is low;
- down: 15 → 14 → 13 → 12 → 11 → 10 → 9; /BORROW only at 0.

Before this, the event model counted 12 → 13 → 14 → 15 → 0 with /CARRY only
at 9. On the board that means IC91 preset to 12 now carries after two UP
clocks (at 13) and then runs on from 4, instead of carrying after the
wrap at 9. In the upper-shape check below IC91 carries 1 454 times over
six frames instead of 1 304. The timer can preset IC75 to 11, which counts
down 11 → 10 → 9 as before.

## Check

```sh
python3 scripts/extract_proms.py
sh sim/74192/run.sh     # needs iverilog
```

0. `sim/74192/tb_states.v`: both cells against the table above, all 16
   states up and down, with /CARRY and /BORROW. 0 errors.
1. `sim/74192/tb_cell.v`: the event and sync cells side by side, alone and
   as a carry/borrow cascade, 200 000 clocks of random count, load and clear.
   0 mismatches.
2. `sim/74192/tb_upper_shape.v`: the unchanged `fonz_upper_shape` is built
   once with the event `fonz_74192` and once with
   `sim/74192/shim_74192_sync.v` (the sync cell behind the old name). Six
   frames (1 421 928 crystal clocks, 1 377 loads, 1 454 IC91 carries) of
   stand-in road, oscillator and line-rate inputs. The traces of IC69/91 Q,
   both carries, IC90, IC73, IC51, the PROM address and the IC71 outputs are
   identical (138 987 changes).

3. `sim/74192/tb_timer.v`: IC75/76 with the loop gates, 9 000 seconds-clock
   half periods, preset select switched and the extended-play latch dropped
   near the end. Event model with the zero-delay loop against the sync cell
   with IC82.13 registered: the same 3 056 time values in the same order;
   the 44 reloads each land one clk later and nothing else moves.

A mutation (presenting the registered `q` instead of the combinational one)
makes the first two checks fail, so they do see a one-clock lag.
